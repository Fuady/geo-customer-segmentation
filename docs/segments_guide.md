# Segment Personas Guide

Detailed profiles, marketing use cases, and targeting recommendations for each geo-behavioral segment.

---

## [0] Urban Commuter

**Description:** High-mobility daily commuter using public transit, with consistent peak-hour data spikes on their commute route.

| Trait | Value |
|---|---|
| Avg Daily Distance | 18–28 km |
| Transit POI Score | High (>0.65) |
| Peak-Hour Data | 40–55% of daily usage |
| ARPU | $70–90 |

**Marketing Strategy:**
- Channel: SMS campaigns timed for commute hours (7–9am, 5–7pm)
- Offer: Unlimited data with priority network during peak hours
- Upsell: Multi-device plan (tablet/laptop for commuting)
- Retain: Alert them when network issues affect their commute route

---

## [1] Home Office Pro

**Description:** Predominantly home-based heavy data user. Video calls, large file transfers, cloud collaboration define their usage. Works from home most days.

| Trait | Value |
|---|---|
| % Time at Home | >70% |
| Data Usage | 35–60 GB/month |
| Afternoon Data Ratio | >38% |
| ARPU | $65–85 |

**Marketing Strategy:**
- Channel: Email campaigns (daytime delivery, weekdays)
- Offer: Fixed-mobile convergence bundle (home broadband + mobile)
- Upsell: Business-grade SLA, video call priority tier
- Retain: Proactive outreach during any home zone outages

---

## [2] Digital Nomad

**Description:** Wide travel radius, frequent roaming, no fixed work location. High entropy mobility pattern with visits to diverse POI types.

| Trait | Value |
|---|---|
| Travel Radius | >50 km |
| Roaming Days | >5/month |
| Frequent Locations | >6 |
| ARPU | $85–110 |

**Marketing Strategy:**
- Channel: App push notifications (most likely to see them)
- Offer: Global roaming bundle with multi-zone data
- Upsell: International SIM, eSIM for travel flexibility
- Retain: Roaming bill shock alerts, auto-bundle notifications

---

## [3] Weekend Explorer

**Description:** Stable weekday patterns with a dramatic shift on weekends — high mobility, leisure POIs, entertainment venues. Uses very little data Mon–Fri.

| Trait | Value |
|---|---|
| Weekend Data Ratio | >50% |
| Avg Daily Dist (weekday) | Low (<8 km) |
| Entertainment POI Score | Medium-High |
| ARPU | $45–65 |

**Marketing Strategy:**
- Channel: Friday afternoon SMS/push before the weekend
- Offer: Weekend data boost add-on (double speed Sat-Sun)
- Upsell: Family data sharing plan for weekend outings
- Retain: Highlight weekend coverage quality at frequent destinations

---

## [4] Social Hub

**Description:** Dense shopping mall and entertainment POI visits. Evening and weekend power users with high SMS/social activity. Highly social lifestyle.

| Trait | Value |
|---|---|
| Mall POI Score | >0.70 |
| Entertainment POI Score | >0.65 |
| Evening Data Ratio | >40% |
| ARPU | $40–60 |

**Marketing Strategy:**
- Channel: Social media ads (Instagram/TikTok), in-mall digital screens
- Offer: Unlimited social apps + streaming (Instagram, TikTok, Netflix)
- Upsell: Family/group plan with shared social perks
- Retain: Seasonal promotions tied to shopping festivals (Harbolnas, etc.)

---

## [5] Senior Steady

**Description:** Minimal mobility, voice-heavy, very low data. Values simplicity and reliability. Lives close to home and rarely travels far.

| Trait | Value |
|---|---|
| % Time at Home | >78% |
| Call Minutes | >450/month |
| Data Usage | <5 GB/month |
| ARPU | $30–45 |

**Marketing Strategy:**
- Channel: Physical retail (store visits), outbound calls
- Offer: Voice-heavy plan with simple flat-rate pricing, no complexity
- Upsell: Emergency data add-on, large-button device bundle
- Retain: Loyalty rewards for long tenure, senior helpline

---

## [6] Young Professional

**Description:** Balanced work and entertainment POI affinity. Moderate commute, growing data needs, evening entertainment and streaming. Early career.

| Trait | Value |
|---|---|
| Office + Transit POI | Medium-High |
| Entertainment POI | Medium-High |
| Evening Usage | 35–42% |
| ARPU | $55–75 |

**Marketing Strategy:**
- Channel: Digital-first (app, social media), workplace wellness programmes
- Offer: Mid-tier unlimited plan with streaming perks (Spotify/Netflix)
- Upsell: International roaming (business travel, holidays)
- Retain: Loyalty tier with career milestone upgrades
